# behind the wrapper
## wagyu, xmr1 and the architecture of trust

research observation date / 25 november 2026

this archive explains a system, not a suspected person. a monero wrapper has two sides: native coins held outside hyperliquid, and a public token traded on hyperliquid. the operator connects them. a token balance proves neither the native reserve nor a completed payout.

the november records reproduce 5.2 million xmr1 transferred from the issuer, followed by market sales. current metadata also reproduces a different hxmr token, deployed on 22 november, with genesis inventory assigned to a separate vault. both the hxmr deployer and that vault return a two-of-three signer configuration. this is a meaningful change in observable control, not a reserve audit or a guarantee of signer independence.

frogman remains a separate incident. the preserved 36-account observations show 3,586.887420 xmr1, unchanged through the earlier 25 november cutoff. those holdings do not identify a controller of the wagyu incident.
