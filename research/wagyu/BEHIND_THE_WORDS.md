# behind the words

| term | plain-language meaning | what it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| wrapper | a tradable representation intended to connect to another asset | that the connection is automatic or fully backed |
| native monero | actual xmr on the monero network | that a similarly named token is xmr |
| genesis inventory | tokens assigned when a token is created | monero received from customers |
| deployer | the account recorded as creating a token | an identified person or the reserve custodian |
| multisig | a threshold of authorized account signatures | that keys belong to independent people or separate secure systems |
| circulating supply | an api accounting field | a verified list of customer claims or reserve liabilities |
| reserve | underlying assets available to meet obligations | token inventory or market capitalization |
| redemption | an actual conversion/payout process | a sale, token transfer or promise to pay |
| proceeds | what an execution or conversion receives | profit, recovered funds or total victim losses |
| gross / net | before / after specifically identified fees | that every other cost has been accounted for |
| market value | quantity multiplied by a selected price | money available for immediate withdrawal |
| bid depth | displayed quantities buyers currently offer to buy | a guaranteed fill or final proceeds |
| compensation | payment addressing an acknowledged loss or claim | any transfer from an operator-linked address |
| snapshot | a balance observation at a stated time | uninterrupted history or continuing eligibility |
| correlation | observations move together or share a service | common identity, coordination or guilt |

monero privacy makes public reserve and payout inspection difficult. selective disclosure, view-key evidence and independent attestations may provide scoped evidence; privacy is not proof that verification is fundamentally impossible. no such reserve attestation was obtained here.
