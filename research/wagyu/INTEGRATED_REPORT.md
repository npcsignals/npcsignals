# behind the wrapper / integrated historical report

## the system before the incident

wagyu presents a service connecting native monero with tokens and cross-chain swaps. a formal founding date was not independently established. the operator's october statement refers to roughly a year of operation; this is an operating-duration assertion, not incorporation evidence. the earliest deployment fact reproduced here is xmr1's returned 17 december 2025 deployment date.

xmr1 began as a 20-million-unit hip-1 genesis allocation to its issuer account. that is public inventory creation. it is not proof that 20 million monero existed behind it. historical documentation describes the operator receiving native deposits and crediting tokens, and making native payouts when tokens are returned. the public trading network can verify its own balances and transfers; the native custody and payment connection requires separate evidence.

## october: exposure and a separate wallet investigation

frogman's october research reaches selected cross-chain routes and matched hyperliquid credits, with explicit upstream provenance limits. its later 36-account set is an exposure inventory, not a list of named perpetrators. a separate 28 october wagyu statement acknowledges a data disclosure. public summaries concern order-related privacy; no leaked customer database was accessed for this archive. neither that disclosure nor frogman's wallet access method is established as the cause of the november event.

## november: inventory moves and a market collapses

first-party issuer and seller ledgers match three transfers totaling 5.2 million xmr1 at 04:46:58.926, 05:14:30.952 and 05:16:10.436 utc on 20 november. the receiving account made seven side transfers totaling 6,000 xmr1 and sold into the market. this proves the recorded account actions, not their authorization or a human identity.

raw paginated fill records now resolve an earlier collection gap. the initial api responses with explicit endTime were empty; no-endTime requests and overlapping pagination recover 4,594 unique fills across markets. small pre-funding test trades are kept apart from the main funded sales. 369,622.92 xmr1 sold after funding through 13:00 utc yields about 13.508 million usdc gross, consistent with the external rounded 369,623 / 13.51 million observation. later sales increase the time-window total to 374,843.15 xmr1 and 13,513,203.749996 usdc gross; neither gross nor net sales are a separate additional loss each time the value moves.

the saved official candle record independently shows the collapse: the 04:48 minute closes at 493.63 usdc; the 05:18 minute reaches 0.872 and closes at 0.99. those are minute intervals, not fabricated millisecond trade times. underlying monero and other similarly named tokens are distinct assets.

## following the proceeds without overstating completeness

system sends, swaps, bridge deposits and destination balances must be treated as successive stages. current fixed-block observations reproduce approximately 7,348 eth in three chain/address positions. sampled evm transactions have successful receipts and block inclusion. however, this archive does not independently reproduce every conversion and every bridge settlement. it therefore separates directly observed holdings from a fully allocated proceeds trail.

## explanation, compensation and replacement

wagyu's operator attributes the incident to a private key retained on an old server at 1984.hosting. an available media reproduction of a joint provider/cert-is notice supports a serious hosting incident; a direct forensic causal link to wagyu's key was not obtained. no link to october's data exposure is established.

the operator promised refunds, later reported most affected users paid and payment plans for some large holders. some hxmr transfers are observable, but no complete rule set, snapshot, loss formula or beneficiary-payment mapping was obtained. distributions of tokens are not automatically compensation, and off-platform payments cannot be ruled out from the original account set.

hxmr is a different token, deployed on 22 november. its genesis places inventory in a separate vault; both that vault and the new deployer expose a two-of-three signer configuration. this is an independently observable change in the control architecture. it does not independently demonstrate reserves, signer independence or an automatic conversion right for old xmr1 holders. current operator documents say the old token is closed and no longer accepted as a monero claim, while legacy open orders are directed to support. legal enforceability and complete outstanding obligations are not assessed.

## what remains after pressure

the verified 25 november frogman checkpoint still shows 3,586.887420 xmr1 and no new returned activity, at its original cutoff. historical acquisition cost, marked value, bid-depth illustration and verified realizable value are kept separate. no permanent-loss, recovery or native-redemption conclusion follows automatically.

the strongest conclusion is architectural: public token accounting, issuer inventory control, native reserves, service terms and completed payouts are different evidence domains. understanding their boundaries explains what this investigation can reproduce—and exactly where it must stop.
