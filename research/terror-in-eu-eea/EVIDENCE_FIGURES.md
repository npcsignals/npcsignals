# Evidence figures

The figures use reported attack entries, including completed, failed and foiled entries. The conditional series reallocates entries for a sensitivity comparison. These charts measure reporting counts and public-source linkage.

The 2019 fixed-geography figure removes 64 UK entries. The 2020 revision is a separate observation. [Annual dataset](data/phase22_annual_analysis.csv) · [Source pages and geographic qualifications](audit/phase25/figures/01_2019_2020_geography_ALT.md).

## 2. Later official revisions and conditional redistribution

[Desktop figure](audit/phase25/figures/03_revisions_en.svg) · [Mobile figure](audit/phase25/figures/mobile/03_revisions_en.svg)

| Reporting year | Original fixed EU27 | Later official revision | Conditional scenario |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2020 | 57 | 56 | 56 |
| 2021 | 15 | 18 | 22 |
| 2022 | 28 | — | 50 |
| 2023 | 120 | — | 94 |

A dash indicates no later official revision in this series. The scenario starts with the later revisions for 2020 and 2021. It then moves 26 French separatist entries out of the 2023 reporting cohort: four to 2021 and 22 to 2022, their stated occurrence years. The individual matches and potential overlap are unresolved. Sources: TE-SAT 2023 p. 9 n. 4 and TE-SAT 2024 p. 51 n. 75. [Annual dataset](data/phase22_annual_analysis.csv) · [Cross-year reporting audit](2022/CROSS_YEAR_REPORTING_AUDIT.md).
