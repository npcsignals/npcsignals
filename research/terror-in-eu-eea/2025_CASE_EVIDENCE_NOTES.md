## Source retrieval and statistical units

The two CSV source registers contained 134 unique IDs and 15 blank publication dates. Every registered URL was submitted to the web reader. It returned document or page text for 109 and failed on 25. See the [per-source retrieval ledger](data/2025_source_integrity_phase7.csv). Returned text can be a landing page, partial extraction or cached copy. These are retrieval results, not 109 full-text claim verifications. Failures include timeouts, access restrictions, gateway errors and size limits. They do not establish permanent link rot. Registered URLs and prior access dates are retained.

Direct retrieval of the full Europol PDF timed out on two attempts; the Publications Office listing opened, but is for the executive summary and has different metadata. No fresh inspection of the full PDF occurred. The statistical audit compares the preserved primary-source transcription and published locators, with the Eucrim summary as a derivative consistency check. Reopening the exact full edition and preserving its bytes and hash were outside that retrieval check. The CRGN catalogue identifies January 2026 as the issue month; this date has been added with month precision. No placeholder dates were filled for the remaining blanks. Catalogue pages and rolling agency pages may lack publication dates or mix multiple editions.

Source origin governs weight. Official press releases republished by wire services may preserve a primary statement (Essen), but the repository does not hold the court's signed reasons. ORF, WDR, RTÉ and newspaper court coverage remain secondary, including when quoting a spokesperson. The Senate's account of PNAT reporting is official parliamentary evidence of that attributed account, not independent confirmation of PNAT's underlying case file. Europol syndications and ministry summaries derived from TE-SAT add no independent proof of an individual EU match. This dated retrieval check does not independently validate quotations from the full report.
