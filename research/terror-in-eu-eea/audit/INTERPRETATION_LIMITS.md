# Interpretation limits

- The annual statistical tables support comparisons of reported entries within the named edition, population and counting unit. Fixed EU27 removes the documented UK rows and retains differences in national classification and inclusion.
- Official revisions, alternative chart readings and the conditional occurrence-year scenario are different observations. The scenario depends on cohort membership and unresolved event overlap.
- The 31-country grid sets the research area. Coverage is partial, and UK, EFTA and EU sources cannot be pooled as equivalent reporting populations. Unidentified entries are cases without adequate public linkage in this archive.
- Casualty figures retain their definition, date and source. The underlying injury definitions and the 2016 conflict preclude a pooled, harmonised injury trend. Counts of arrests, verdicts and perpetrators refer to distinct populations.
- Judicial conclusions apply to their specific decisions and procedural stages. The evidence on membership, claims of responsibility, communication, financing and assistance is case-specific. The records do not support a cross-case command trend or a comprehensive financial-support series.
- Authority-level comparisons and selected court records illustrate institutional differences. They do not establish representative patterns across all events.
