# Annual evidence notes, 2015–2024

These notes record source observations and coverage limits from the annual audits. The linked baseline, case and proceeding registers carry the underlying evidence. Counts are original reporting entries.

## 2015

The occupation of slots is a lower bound for the November Paris series. Exact site-to-slot keys are still missing. The French parliamentary inquiry is primary for attributed investigation findings, not a substitute for criminal judgments.

The [provenance register](../2015/provenance_evidence.csv) preserves the unexplained **193** shown in TE-SAT 2018 p. 9 Fig. 1 for 2015. The original edition, TE-SAT 2017 and TE-SAT 2019 each show **211**. No correction notice, revised country table or underlying allocation was located. The alternate chart value is neither inserted in the original series nor used to calculate a revised geography.
