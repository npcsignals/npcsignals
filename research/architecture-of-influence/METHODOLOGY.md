# methodology and scope

This investigation separates the content available to a platform, content eligible to be recommended, content ranked or delivered, content actually watched, and the meanings or beliefs people take from it. These are different quantities. A change at one stage does not establish a change at the next.

## evidence classifications

- **CONFIRMED**: a primary source, public filing or preserved file directly states the fact recorded. This classification confirms the statement or record, not every interpretation of it.
- **SUPPORTED**: an empirical result or conclusion is reported by a source whose relevant text or abstract was checked. Unless the evidence register says otherwise, NPCsignals did not recompute the result from raw data.
- **UNRESOLVED**: available evidence is insufficient, conflicting or inaccessible for the proposed claim.
- **NOT ASSESSED**: outside this review’s scope.

Each row also has a verification level: bibliographic, abstract, full-text, supplementary-material, computational reproduction or independent empirical replication. These levels are not interchangeable. No TikTok research result in this archive was independently empirically replicated. No author dataset/code for the cited science studies was computationally rerun.

## comparison logic

The investigation does not infer ranking effects from a high topic share alone. Creator supply, popularity, language, geography, user activity, network structure and classifier performance are treated as alternative explanations. German studies are not pooled: the Tjaden automated audit and Wedel donated histories have distinct samples, methods and denominators. Earlier and final versions of the Tjaden article are one research line, not independent evidence.

The transparency correction comparison checks file hashes, row shape, category keys, duplicate headers and values. Missing fields remain distinct from numeric zero. Parent/child arithmetic is reported without assuming that published categories must be additive.

## scope exclusions

The disputed German weekly arithmetic example is excluded. The two Guess et al. correction notices are recorded, but their inaccessible full text prevents using precise result claims. Litigation allegations about internal documents are not accepted as established facts without the underlying documents, their context and provenance. No product model is inferred from a patent, provider research paper or company description alone.

## reader language

“Exposure” refers to observed or recorded appearance/watching, not persuasion. “Visibility” is not public agreement. “Preliminary finding” is not a final infringement decision. A published commitment is not evidence that a safeguard works. Conceptual and synthetic figures are labelled separately from archived report counts.
